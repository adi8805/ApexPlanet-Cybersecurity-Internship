**Social Engineering**  
_Hacking the human, not the machine._

- **How:** Manipulation, deception, or intimidation to get people to break security protocols.
    
- **Methods:** Phishing (digital), vishing (voice), tailgating (physical), pretexting (fake scenarios).
    
- **CIA Impact:** **Confidentiality** (credential theft), **Integrity** (tricking users into altering data), **Availability** (ransomware deployment).
    
- **Defense:** Security awareness training, verification protocols (e.g., call-back to known number), strict physical access policies.

**Wireless Attacks**  
_Targeting radio-frequency networks (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, RFID)._

- **How:** Intercepting, eavesdropping, or injecting malicious traffic over the air.
    
- **Types:**
    
    - **Evil Twin:** Fake Wi-Fi hotspot to steal credentials.
        
    - **Packet Sniffing:** Capturing unencrypted data in transit.
        
    - **Bluesnarfing:** Unauthorized access via Bluetooth.
        
    - **Deauthentication Attack:** Kicking users off to force reconnection to a rogue AP.
        
- **CIA Impact:** **Confidentiality** (data interception) & **Availability** (deauth floods).
    
- **Defense:** WPA3 encryption, VPNs, disabling unused radios, MAC filtering, rogue AP detection.

**Insider Threats**  
_Risk originating from within the organization._

- **How:** Employees, contractors, or partners with authorized access who misuse it.
    
- **Types:**
    
    - **Malicious:** Disgruntled employees stealing/selling data.
        
    - **Negligent:** Accidental exposure (e.g., misaddressed email, weak passwords).
        
    - **Compromised:** User's account taken over by an external attacker.
        
- **CIA Impact:** **All 3** – data leaks (Confidentiality), data tampering (Integrity), or deleting critical systems (Availability).
    
- **Defense:** Least-privilege access, User Behavior Analytics (UBA), Data Loss Prevention (DLP), exit interviews, monitoring privileged users, clear security policies.
