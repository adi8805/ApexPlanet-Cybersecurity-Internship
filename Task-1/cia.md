**CIA Triad**  
Core security model for protecting information. Foundation of all security programs.

---

**Confidentiality**  
_Keeping secrets secret._

- Prevents unauthorized access to data.
    
- **Threats:** Hacking, eavesdropping, theft.
    
- **Controls:** Encryption, access controls (passwords, MFA), data classification.
    

---

**Integrity**  
_Data is accurate and trustworthy._

- Prevents unauthorized modification or deletion.
    
- **Threats:** Hackers altering data, human error, system glitches.
    
- **Controls:** Hashing (checksums), digital signatures, audit logs, version control.
    

---

**Availability**  
_Data is accessible when needed._

- Ensures systems and data are up and running.
    
- **Threats:** DDoS attacks, hardware failure, ransomware, power outages.
    
- **Controls:** Redundancy, backups, disaster recovery plans, failover systems.
